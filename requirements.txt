  Risk Management

  Technical Risks

  1. Performance at Scale
  - Risk: System degradation with millions of documents
  - Mitigation: Implement sharding, caching, CDN from day one

  2. Real-time Collaboration Complexity
  - Risk: Conflict resolution in concurrent editing
  - Mitigation: Use proven CRDT libraries (Yjs, Automerge)

  3. Search Accuracy
  - Risk: Poor relevance in search results
  - Mitigation: Implement ML-based ranking, user feedback loops

  4. Security Vulnerabilities
  - Risk: Data breaches, unauthorized access
  - Mitigation: Security audits, penetration testing, SOC 2 compliance

  Business Risks

  1. Feature Creep
  - Risk: Scope expansion delaying launch
  - Mitigation: Strict MVP definition, phased releases

  2. Adoption Challenges
  - Risk: Users resistant to new platform
  - Mitigation: Migration tools, training programs, gradual rollout

  3. Competition
  - Risk: Market changes, new competitors
  - Mitigation: Focus on differentiators (AI, modern UX)
